Anthropic names the exact interface tradeoff its prompt-cache design forces on you.

Most in-conversation settings render ahead of your content into the cached prefix, so changing them mid-conversation breaks the cache — with one exception: on Claude Opus 5 and Fable 5.1 you can now update reasoning effort mid-conversation without that cost. Rather than reordering tool lists dynamically, Anthropic recommends declaring every tool upfront and marking rare ones defer_loading, entering the conversation only when the model looks them up.

On effort: too low and "Claude stops before it has enough evidence," too high and it "spends more time deliberating than the task warrants" — an argument for per-task tuning over a universal default. The team's own account, verified verbatim against the live page.

Microsoft's design-system team found API docs nearly useless for guiding AI-assisted design — intent is what agents actually lack.

Jenny Lanier Wolski, Principal Design Manager on Microsoft's CoreAI team, writes that Azure/Foundry products "felt disjointed" even while reusing the same atomic components; testing found "agents can often understand component APIs directly from installed packages and TypeScript definitions. What they struggle with is intent."

The fix is two parallel tracks rather than one shared artifact: a Figma library for designers, and an MCP server carrying "a context layer" of references and metadata built for how agents interpret guidance. Documentation written for people cannot double as guidance for a generating agent — a design system now needs two guidance layers, not one reused across both audiences.

Making a robot deliver something physically only helps immersion if the user already likes robots.

A between-subjects HRI 2026 study (N=67) of a robot game-master found no overall effect of physical versus digital delivery — until split by pre-existing attitude toward robots. Participants wary of robots lost immersion under tangible delivery while comfortable participants saw no drop; the authors conclude "the digital interface acts as a protective social buffer" for anxious users.

That kills a common default: upgrading a digital cue to a physical one isn't a safe universal improvement — infer robot-attitude first, or keep a digital fallback. Single peer-reviewed source, six months old, kept as a durable interaction finding.

A 0.6-billion-parameter model now transcribes overlapping speakers in just over a second, at near-single-speaker accuracy.

NVIDIA's new streaming architecture hits 1.04 seconds of latency — matching its own single-speaker baseline — while cutting multi-speaker word-error rate to 16.18 versus 45.25 for a conventional diarize-then-transcribe pipeline, degrading single-speaker accuracy only marginally (7.44 vs. 7.16 WER).

That makes real-time multi-party meeting captioning viable as one small edge model, rather than a diarization service bolted onto a separate ASR pipeline. Single-source preprint, accepted to Interspeech 2026, figures confirmed against the paper's own table.

OpenAI's new Agents API hands you context compaction and session recovery as a managed primitive.

The public beta pairs a "managed Codex harness" with server-side session orchestration: durable sessions "continue work across turns, stream progress, and connect your own tools and MCP servers," in OpenAI-hosted sandboxes or your own infrastructure.

A genuinely long-running background agent no longer requires the designer to build custom logic for what gets dropped when context fills up — that's now a vendor-managed default. Confirmed on the changelog, corroborated independently by three outlets.

The framing theory also doesn't do what the digest implied — Bandura's modes of agency concern influencing other people, with no category for delegation, the behavior this paper mostly documents.
